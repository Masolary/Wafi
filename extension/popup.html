Reader Guard 1.1.2

For NovelCool chapters and novel listings.

Checking protection…

Grant this extension access to novelcool.com in Safari, then reload your novel page.

Use the green reading toolbar for chapter links and clean reading.

What changes?

Blocks page JavaScript, embedded frames and plugins on /chapter/ and /novel/ pages. Other NovelCool pages are unchanged. Comments, ratings and some menus may not work while protected.

No analytics, accounts, paid services or remote code. Only your font size and reading theme are saved locally.

Turning protection off requires a page reload. It does not bypass age checks, logins or paywalls.
